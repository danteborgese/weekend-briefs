🗺️ Field Trip

Sunday morning · Oct 4 · 8:00 AM start

🚲 Tour de Bronx

The 32nd annual Tour de Bronx, billed as the largest free cycling event in New York State. Choose a 10-mile family-friendly, 25-mile or 40-mile route through the Bronx (South Bronx, Wakefield, Riverdale, Orchard Beach and waterfront parks). All routes finish at the New York Botanical Garden.

📍 StartEast 161st St & Grand Concourse, Bronx
⏰ Time8:00 AM. The 40-mile group goes first, the 25-mile group about 15 minutes later, then the 10-mile group
🏁 FinishNew York Botanical Garden, with an end-of-ride music festival (routes conclude at 4 PM per the Bronx Times)
💵 PriceFree
🗺️ RoutesReleased around 6 AM Sunday. Streets are open to traffic, with NYPD at key intersections
🔧 Good to knowRest stops with water, snacks, restrooms and first aid. Bike rentals via Unlimited Biking. No parking at the start or finish.

Registration: Registration is through the Bronx Tourism Council (Eventbrite). Early bib pickup ran Oct 1 and Oct 3 (8 AM to 12 PM, closed now), so check the official page for what is needed on Sunday morning.

Rain note: Showers are possible after 11am (50%), so start with the first wave and aim to finish early. The 10-mile or 25-mile route is the safest bet if you want to beat the rain.
